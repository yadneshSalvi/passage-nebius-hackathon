# Journey Compiler evaluation protocol

Protocol v1, 28 October 2026. This is a ready protocol for future independent evaluation, not a report of participants, holdouts or comparative wins. No suitable independent people were available during implementation. All current external cases remain development cases.

## Freeze before admitting final incidents

Record a versioned manifest of prompts, exact provider/model/profile, runner and dependency hashes, contract vocabulary, task/adapter capabilities, token and wall-clock allowances, candidate allowance, reviewer instructions and inclusion rules. Freeze that manifest before inspecting final cases. A later protocol change creates a new series; it does not silently replace an earlier failure.

Admit twelve defective incidents from at least three independently authored applications, with development repositories and shared component ancestry excluded. Add four independently checked sound interactions as a separate control set. A sound interaction left unchanged is not a repaired defect. Public historical bugs must disclose possible training exposure. Unsupported builds, inaccessible services and missing business adapters are reported as admission/environment outcomes, with predefined rules for whether they enter the repair denominator.

Maintain one row per admitted incident: repository/commit/subtree, permissions, task, independently authored expected outcome, public observations, adapter effort, model attempts/usage, selected candidate, final result, environment errors, regression outcome and reviewer decision. Repeated generations remain repeats of the same incident, not additional independent applications.

## Independent obligations and matched comparison

A maintainer confirms the business objective and an accessibility reviewer supplies temporal/recovery requirements before seeing the model's draft or patch. Their sealed obligations and final outcome checks live outside the repairable source, model context and public feedback. A draft can omit an important requirement even if every emitted assertion passes; score that omission explicitly.

Compare full Passage with a competent general coding agent using the same exact model/provider/profile, source revision, task and business information, public browser/accessibility tools, candidate limit, token ceiling and elapsed-time allowance. Keep final obligations hidden from both. Select one candidate per trial before opening final results. If using replicates, retain case-level grouping. Temporal-contract and actual-AT-feedback ablations require their own predeclared conditions. Do not fabricate a proprietary-tool comparator without actual access.

Report raw counts first: complete valid task repairs, critical false acceptance, preserved tasks, sound controls changed unnecessarily, failures/timeouts, reviewer acceptance and measured adapter/review time. The proposed small-pilot gates are eight valid repairs of twelve and two additional valid cases over the matched baseline, or a measured authoring/review benefit with equal correctness. These are engineering decisions, not statistical dominance claims.

## Contract quality and revision protection

Have independent reviewers score the first ten unseen drafts, recording each substantive correction, ambiguity, omitted obligation and weakened requirement. The planned gate is at least eight approved drafts needing at most one substantive correction, with no weakened business obligation reaching execution. Current TodoMVC development drafting needed three substantive removals and therefore is not evidence that this gate passed.

For every retained journey guard, use the same frozen contract against later source revisions. Require two independently introduced harmless refactors to pass and two relevant regressions to fail. Preserve source hashes and actual failure signatures. Changes that intentionally alter the task require new review; they cannot be disguised as harmless refactors. Current authored development checks satisfy that limited four-revision exercise, not a cross-repository longitudinal study.

## AT configuration and participant study

Freeze each exact browser/AT/OS/image configuration and keyboard protocol. Run twenty independent startups against an unchanged known-good build. The planned reliability gate is nineteen completed captures of twenty; all failures remain in the denominator. Classify environment startup failure separately from an application task failure. Any runtime fix or timeout change creates a separately reported series. Do not retry a failed run invisibly or pool successful runs from different configurations.

Actual screen-reader speech is still automation. A participant study requires real people, informed consent, a suitable task and withdrawal/deletion handling. Assign matched tasks with counterbalanced order, retain every assigned task including stops, and record assistance/rescue, completion, newly observed barriers and participant comments. Create each protected application session before the attempt. Compare exact expected business fields with that attempt's record; never reuse an automated verifier booking.

A separately named reviewer may attest to a captured session and outcome. In the local product this is an attestation, not an authenticated identity or proof of independence. Report that limit. No automated capture may increase participant counts. The planned benefit gate is a majority completing a previously blocked matched task without rescue and no new critical observed barrier; no such result is currently claimed.

## Maintainer and first-use review

Ask three independent maintainers to review a patch and runnable test without substantive rewriting; record acceptance and actual adoption separately. Ask five unfamiliar evaluators to explain the task/result within thirty seconds and complete the guided interaction within two minutes without coaching. Record every attempt and confusion. Product screenshots, axe scans and the development team's walkthroughs do not stand in for these human outcomes.

The intended gate is two of three maintainers accepting and four of five unfamiliar evaluators meeting the first-use target. If people are unavailable, keep these rows pending rather than producing synthetic participants, inferred endorsements or numerical benefit estimates.
